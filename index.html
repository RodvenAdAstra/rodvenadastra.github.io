I am a lawyer and investment banker with more than 18 years in financial structuring, M&A and capital raising across Latin America and the United States. I have worked on transactions exceeding USD 300 million in energy, pharma, real assets and real estate.

My job is to turn a company or project into a financeable opportunity: organize the information, identify risks before the bank or fund does, design the right structure and support the process through closing.
